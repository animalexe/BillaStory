Lore / direction

A world built
for stories.

Billo Story is designed as a connected cinematic universe: recurring characters, recognizable places, short-form animation and longer episodes living under one visual identity.

01 • Characters

Personality first.

Every character has a distinct costume, silhouette, pose language and expression library. The website keeps those references together so future scenes remain consistent.

02 • Motion

Small loops, big feel.

Five-second loops are ideal for character intros, section transitions, teasers and social snippets. Full episodes can sit above the same system later.
